Caveats
-------
- The GREEN probe log's head shows sclang compiling the machine's class
  library, including downloaded quarks. The GREEN result is therefore a
  control on the eviction policy, not a quark-free or default-class-library
  baseline. The same applies to the two #7711 control runs summarized in the
  report.
- The application log is from a private application; its source is not linked
  publicly. Only the two error excerpts above are included.
- No per-run logs were retained for the 20/25 capacity-192 series, the capacity
  sweep, or the tail-removal count. Those are historical recorded outcomes in
  the report, not retained-log measurements.
